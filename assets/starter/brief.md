# Production brief: A small idea, shared

- Audience: a general audience, with no specialist vocabulary assumed.
- Takeaway: an idea becomes more useful through exploration, making, connection and sharing.
- Input: an original fictional story; no real-world metrics or project claims.
- Language: English, with a calm conversational narrator.
- Format: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, approximately one minute; actual speech determines duration.
- Recurring element: one amber lantern follows a continuous path between miniature islands.
- Direction: cream stone, sage planting, terracotta accents, soft daylight and orthographic architecture.
- Chapters: a first spark → a new perspective → pieces fit together → a bridge carries the work → a shared garden.
- Outputs: narrated film, chapter clips, voice tracks, subtitles, cover/keyframes, PPTX/PDF/ODP, speaker notes, review website and production archive.
- Review state: inspect rendered motion and listen before presenting a finished production as accepted.
